### Stop MiniAudio from killing music to make room for sound effects
- On the Pi the menu music still stopped after the loop fix, usually once the shell map battle got busy.
- When the 2D or 3D sample budget is full, `SoundManager` asks `isPlayingLowerPriority()` for a sound to replace, and the new sound kills that handle. ### Loop music like Miles in the MiniAudio and OpenAL backends
- First playable session on the Raspberry Pi 5. Sound effects were fine, but menu and in-game music started and then went silent.
- Cause: Miles plays music streams with `INFINITE_LOOP_COUNT`, and map scripts move to the next track through `hasMusicTrackCompleted()`, which counts completed loops. MiniAudio and OpenAL played each track once and released it, and `hasMusicTrackCompleted()` always returned FALSE, so nothing ever started the next track.
- MiniAudio: music sounds are now set to loop. `processPlayingList()` counts loops by watching the PCM cursor wrap back to the start, and `hasMusicTrackCompleted()` compares that count the way Miles does.
- OpenAL (parity): finished music streams are rewound by reopening the file in the same `FFmpegFile` (`restartMusicStream()`), loops are counted, and `hasMusicTrackCompleted()` uses the count.
